# 04 - Ideation

## Proposed Idea

LegalEase is an AI-powered legal document drafting application that helps users create structured legal-document drafts from simple inputs.

The application combines a simple web interface with Google's Gemini AI to generate document content based on the information provided by the user.

## Key Ideas

### 1. Simple Document Selection

Users can select the type of legal document they want to create.

Examples:

- Employment Contract
- Service Agreement
- Confidentiality Agreement
- Other supported legal documents

### 2. Guided Information Input

Users provide important information such as:

- Document type
- Names of parties
- Roles of parties
- Terms and conditions
- Effective date
- Other relevant details

### 3. AI-Assisted Drafting

The provided information is sent to the AI model, which generates a structured legal-document draft.

### 4. Document Preview

Users can review the generated document before downloading it.

### 5. Multiple Export Formats

The generated document can be exported into supported formats such as:

- TXT
- DOCX
- PDF

## Expected Benefit

The proposed solution aims to reduce the effort required to create an initial legal-document draft while providing a simple and accessible user experience.

## Safety Consideration

AI-generated legal documents should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being used for actual legal purposes.
