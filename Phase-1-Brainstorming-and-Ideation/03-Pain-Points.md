# 03 - Pain Points

## Main User Pain Points

### 1. Time-Consuming Drafting

Creating a legal document manually can require significant time for researching, writing, formatting, and reviewing content.

### 2. Lack of Legal Knowledge

Many users are unfamiliar with legal terminology, document structures, and commonly used clauses.

### 3. Missing Important Information

Users may accidentally leave out important details such as:

- Party information
- Effective dates
- Payment terms
- Responsibilities
- Confidentiality requirements
- Termination conditions

### 4. Difficult Formatting

Creating a professional-looking legal document manually can be challenging, especially when users need consistent formatting.

### 5. Multiple Export Requirements

Users may need their documents in different formats such as:

- TXT
- DOCX
- PDF

Preparing the same document in multiple formats manually adds extra work.

### 6. Difficulty Getting Started

Users may know what document they need but may not know how to structure the document or what information should be included.

## Opportunity

LegalEase can address these pain points by providing a simple interface where users enter basic information and receive a structured legal-document draft that can be reviewed and exported.
