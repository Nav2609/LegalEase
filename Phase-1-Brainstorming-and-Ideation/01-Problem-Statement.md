# 01 - Problem Statement

## Problem

Creating legal documents manually can be time-consuming and difficult for users who are not familiar with legal drafting.

Users often need to prepare documents such as employment contracts, agreements, notices, and other legal drafts. The process usually requires understanding legal language, organizing information correctly, and ensuring that important details are included.

## Current Challenges

- Manual legal document drafting takes significant time.
- Users may not know the correct legal structure or terminology.
- Important clauses or information may be missed.
- Formatting professional legal documents can be difficult.
- Converting drafts into downloadable document formats requires additional effort.

## Proposed Problem to Solve

LegalEase aims to simplify the initial legal-document drafting process by allowing users to provide basic information and generate a structured legal document draft using AI.

The system should make document creation faster, easier, and more accessible while clearly communicating that generated documents should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before use.
