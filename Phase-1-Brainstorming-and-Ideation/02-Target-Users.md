# 02 - Target Users

## Primary Users

### 1. Individuals

People who need to create basic legal documents but may not have legal drafting knowledge.

Examples:
- Employees
- Employers
- Freelancers
- Small business owners
- Individuals creating agreements

### 2. Small Businesses

Small businesses that need to prepare common legal documents quickly without maintaining a dedicated legal drafting team.

Examples:
- Employment contracts
- Service agreements
- Business agreements
- Confidentiality agreements

### 3. Students and Developers

Students and developers who want to understand how AI can be used to assist with structured legal-document generation.

## User Needs

Users should be able to:

- Select the type of legal document they need.
- Enter basic information about the parties.
- Provide relevant terms and conditions.
- Specify an effective date.
- Generate a structured legal-document draft.
- Preview the generated document.
- Export the document into common formats.

## Important Note

LegalEase is intended to assist with document drafting and does not replace professional legal advice.
