# 06 - User Journey

## Overview

The LegalEase user journey is designed to keep the document-drafting process simple and straightforward.

## User Flow

```text
Open LegalEase
      ↓
Select Document Type
      ↓
Enter Party Information
      ↓
Enter Terms and Conditions
      ↓
Enter Effective Date
      ↓
Click "Generate Document"
      ↓
AI Generates Draft
      ↓
Review Document Preview
      ↓
Download Document
      ↓
TXT / DOCX / PDF
