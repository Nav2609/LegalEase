# 10 - Phase 1 Summary

## Phase Overview

Phase 1 focused on brainstorming and ideation for the LegalEase project.

The main objective was to identify the problem, understand the target users, analyze their pain points, develop the proposed idea, and define the initial project requirements and scope.

## Key Outcomes

### Problem Identified

Manual legal-document drafting can be time-consuming and difficult for users without legal drafting knowledge.

### Target Users Identified

The primary users include:

- Individuals
- Small businesses
- Students
- Developers

### Main Pain Points Identified

- Time-consuming manual drafting
- Lack of legal knowledge
- Missing important information
- Difficult document formatting
- Multiple export requirements
- Difficulty getting started

### Proposed Solution

LegalEase was proposed as an AI-powered application that assists users in creating structured legal-document drafts using Google's Gemini AI.
